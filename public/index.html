A small light for what we keep.

EchoLantern is a private memory-writing tool. It helps shape your own notes into a keepsake; it doesn't verify memories, speak for people, or contact spirits.

Local AI: When running locally with Ollama, your prompt is sent to the Ollama service you configured. On the hosted preview, the app uses a clearly labelled browser template.

Your saved keepsakes and photos stay in this browser's local storage. They're not encrypted. Avoid entering passwords, secrets, or information you aren't comfortable storing here.
